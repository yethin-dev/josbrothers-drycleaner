Terms and Conditions

1. Stain Removal Policy

The company does not utilize specific chemical treatments or specialized processes for stain removal. As the complete removal of stains cannot be guaranteed, all items are processed according to standard cleaning protocols.

2. Limitation of Liability for Material Defects

While all articles are handled with the utmost care, the company is not responsible for discoloration, shrinkage, or damage resulting from non-apparent or inherent material defects. Such items are accepted for service strictly at the owner's risk.

3. Embellishments and Manufacturing Issues

The company shall incur no liability for damage, shrinkage, color bleeding, or general deterioration caused by the nature of the fabric, embellishments (fancy work), or pre-existing manufacturing defects.

4. Inspection and Notification

Articles are subject to a detailed professional inspection at our central processing unit. Any discrepancies regarding the condition of an item will be communicated via the provided contact number within five (5) days. If a response is not received within this period, the company's assessment shall be deemed final and binding.

5. Verification of Articles

Customers are required to inspect all garments and serviced articles at the time of collection or delivery. The company accepts no responsibility for missing items once they have left our premises.

6. Unclaimed Items

Any article not collected within six (6) months of the scheduled due date may be disposed of at the company's discretion without further notice.

7. Right of Refusal

The company reserves the right to decline or cancel a service order even after initial acceptance at a shop, outlet, or counter.

8. Loss or Damage Compensation

In the event of loss or irreparable damage, and as a gesture of goodwill, the company's liability is limited to a maximum of ten (10) times the individual cleaning charge for that specific garment.

9. Processing Methods

The company retains full discretion over the cleaning methods and processes used to achieve satisfactory results. No claims or disputes regarding the chosen process will be entertained after the service is completed.

10. Complaint Window

All complaints must be formally lodged within 48 hours of delivery if the customer accepts the items without prior verification at the outlet or counter.

11. Jurisdiction

Any legal disputes or proceedings arising from these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Ernakulam.

12. Proof of Deposit

Articles will be released and delivered only upon the presentation of the original service receipt.
