Process of Work

INSPECTION & TAGGING

When you bring the fabric to us, your clothes are examined. The cleaner will look out for stains, colour variations and any damages. If there are any stains spotted, whether its ink, blood or nasty grease, they note it and tag it.

SORTED AND DRYCLEANED

The clothes are then colour separated and placed in the machine or hand dipped in MTO (mineral turpentine oil), submerged in industrial fabric solvents and soap oils. Later, it's spun in a perforated cylinder and the excess solvent is removed. By that time.

DRIED

Once the washing is over with, the fabric is dried, with the garments folded inside-out to prevent colour loss. Using this technique, it helps retain the color and texture of your garments.

PRESSED AND PACKED

For the final touch, your garments are meticulously ironed and steamed, packed with collar holders and bands to utmost perfection, making them look brand new.
